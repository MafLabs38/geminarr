# Runbook

> Operating Geminarr: what to watch, and what to do when it is degraded. It grows with the verticals; a failure mode is added here **in the pull request that introduces it** (the architecture reviewer checks).

## What to watch

| Where                    | What it says                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `GET /health/ready`      | The container can do its job (the data folder is writable). Used by the platform's probe.                                        |
| The overview of the page | The state of the connection, of the link to Mnemarr, and the last runs _(from the verticals that add them)_                      |
| Mnemarr's machines page  | Geminarr as an `always-on` machine: `online`, or `failing` with its sentence, or `silent` after 30 minutes without a heartbeat   |
| The mail from Mnemarr    | "Geminarr needs you to sign in again" (vertical 6), with a link to Mnemarr's Machines page, where the link to Geminarr's page is |

## Reasons and what to do

Codes are in [SPEC.md §10.3](SPEC.md#103-health-and-reason-codes). ## Recover

- **The desktop's window says 401 ("This link to the desktop is not valid any more"), shows nothing (a gateway error, or a blank page over plain HTTP), or Geminarr says `browser-failed`**: a 401 is a ticket that was used, that waited more than a minute, or whose session ended: press _Sign in_ again (the window is opened by the page, on its own origin `GEMINARR__BROWSER__DESKTOPPORT`, which your proxy must publish). Otherwise: the browser container is not started or not reachable. The desktop needs HTTPS or `localhost` to draw its screen. Look at its log and at the `browser` check of the health (the page _Overview_); in Compose Geminarr must run with `network_mode: service:browser`, in Kubernetes in the same pod. Chromium needs about 1 GB of shared memory (`shm_size`) and memory above 1 GB.
- **A lost browser volume** (volume lost, a restore): the state turns `needs-login` (the session is gone); sign in again in the browser (the page _Connection_, _Sign in_). _Disconnect_ on the page _Connection_ clears the cookies of the browser and forgets the connection (`not-connected`).
- **A restored Mnemarr** (fewer lines than Geminarr believes it sent): Geminarr goes back to Mnemarr's positions by itself and sends again what is missing.
- **A suspected leak of the session**: _Disconnect_ in Geminarr is **local**: the extension deletes the cookies of its browser and the connection is forgotten; it revokes nothing at the provider (a cookie copied earlier stays valid, and what the assistant's pages stored in the browser is not cleared). The step that counts is in the dedicated Google account's security page: sign out of all sessions and remove the device, change the password, and sign in again by hand. The browser's volume is the session: nobody else may read it, and its page and its debugging port must never be reachable without a login.
- **A stuck pass**: it stops at `PassTimeoutMinutes` by itself; "Run now" while one runs is refused.
- **Upgrade of the browser** (a new image version, pinned by digest): its profile is its own format; if the state turns `needs-login` after an upgrade, sign in again and write the version here.

## The extension

- **It does not read**: in the browser, `chrome://policy` shows the policy (is it loaded?), `chrome://extensions` shows the extension (installed, enabled, the version). A policy the browser cannot parse installs nothing: check the JSON.
- **The browser did not install it**: open the `update_url` from the browser's container; a 404 is a package that the image lacks (the image was built without the folder: `GEMINARR__EXTENSION__PACKAGEFOLDER`), a 400 is a name that is not allowed.
- **A new version does not arrive**: the browser's own check runs about every five hours; the extension asks every hour. To force it: remove the entry of the policy, restart the browser, put it back.
- **The browser has been silent for days and nobody wrote**: `extension-silent` is a sentence on Mnemarr's machines page (the machine is `failing`), not a mail (SPEC Q-14): look at that page now and then until the first weeks of running say whether it should be one.
- **The signing key was lost or changed**: it is a new extension, with a new id: change the id in the policy of every browser. Packages signed by another key are refused by the browser, so nothing installs by mistake.
- **Rotate the token**: new token in Geminarr's file, then in the policy, then restart the browser. Between the two, `extension-silent` and a `401` in the log.
- **The page of Gemini changed and nothing is read**: the extension reads with the selectors of `extension/content.js`; a change of the page is a new version of the extension (a release). Nothing is lost: the next round reads what was missed.

## Spike hygiene (while a spike is going on)

`.spike/` and `.profile/` are git-ignored and **hold a signed-in session and real conversations**: they live on disk (not in `/tmp`, which is memory on the build machine), are deleted when the spike's result is written down, and a spike's findings written in `docs/spikes/` contain the _shape_ of the pages and never their content.

## Signing in

- **Locked out** (HTTP 429 after five wrong passwords, for fifteen minutes): wait, or restart the container (the lockout is in memory). If it happens without you having tried, someone is guessing the name: change it (`GEMINARR__AUTH__ADMINUSER`).
- **Rotate the password**: replace the content of the file; the next sign-in uses it, and sessions that are open stay valid until their end. **If the password leaked, restart the container after changing it: that ends every session.**
- **Nobody can sign in and the page only says "wrong"**: the log of the container says why at its start (`Nobody can sign in (NotConfigured | PasswordUnreadable | PasswordWeak)`), and the reason codes above say what to do. Each refused sign-in and each lockout is in the log too, without the name or the password typed.
- **A page that answers 400 "The host name is not allowed"**: add the name or the address you reach it by to `GEMINARR__ALLOWEDHOSTNAMES` (or set `GEMINARR__PUBLICURL`).
- **Signed out all of a sudden**: the container restarted (sessions are in memory), or the 12 hours passed.
