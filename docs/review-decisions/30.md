# Review decisions of pull request #30

The answers to the findings of the four review agents on the head commits `79a7fe3` (R-30-1 to R-30-37) `edb5a21` (R-30-38 to R-30-62) `6657155` (R-30-63 to R-30-86) and `b8123e6` (R-30-87 and after). See the [README](README.md).

## R-30-1 | security | high | fixed

- **Finding**: Anyone in the cluster or on the Docker network can reach the browser, whose web desktop has no login and holds the signed-in session.
- **Where**: deploy/kubernetes/browser.example.yaml, deploy/browser/docker-compose.example.yml
- **Reason**: Real. The Kubernetes example denies all ingress to the browser's pod; the Compose example puts the browser on a network of its own shared with Geminarr alone and says to give the desktop its own login.

## R-30-2 | security | medium | fixed

- **Finding**: The policy does not enforce what SECURITY.md promises (only the reader's extension, no debugging port).
- **Where**: deploy/browser/policy.example.json, tests/e2e/stack/extension/prepare.mjs
- **Reason**: Real. `ExtensionSettings` `*` blocked, `DeveloperToolsAvailability` 2, `RemoteDebuggingAllowed` false, and `URLBlocklist` `*` with an `URLAllowlist` of the assistant's origins: in the examples, in the e2e stack (which runs under it) and in the sandbox (the real page was read under it).

## R-30-3 | security | medium | fixed

- **Finding**: The body of a push is read and parsed before the token is checked.
- **Where**: ExtensionController
- **Reason**: Real. The token is checked by an authorization filter, which runs before model binding; a test sends a body that is not JSON without the token and gets a 401.

## R-30-4 | security | low | fixed

- **Finding**: The token travels in clear over plain HTTP.
- **Where**: docs/network.md
- **Reason**: Said in the docs: acceptable on one host or a network of its own, not across nodes that do not encrypt; use an https address (`geminarrUrl`, `EXTENSION__PUBLICURL`) or a mesh otherwise.

## R-30-5 | security | low | fixed

- **Finding**: Non-release builds mount the signing key while reading the layer cache.
- **Where**: ci.yml, Dockerfile
- **Reason**: Real. Only a release build gets the key (and it reads no cache); the others sign with an ephemeral key.

## R-30-6 | security | low | fixed

- **Finding**: A multi-line PEM passed in `secrets:` may be split; nothing compares the published id with the expected one.
- **Where**: ci.yml
- **Reason**: The key is passed with `secret-envs` (an environment variable keeps its lines). The id is compared by the operator (runbook, DEPLOYMENT.md step 2); a check in CI would need the id as a variable that does not exist yet.

## R-30-7 | architecture | major | fixed

- **Finding**: A changed page has no state of its own: the reader can never say `reader-broken`; a renamed link would send `signedIn: false` and a mail "sign in again".
- **Where**: extension/content.js, ExtensionService, ExtensionReader
- **Reason**: Real, and found on the real page too. A page that nobody is signed in to is told by a sign-in link it shows, never by the absence of the other; a page that shows neither, a page of an account without its list, or a conversation without turns says `broken` with a step (page, list, turns: a closed list), and the reader says `reader-broken`. Tests at each level, and an e2e that breaks the fake page.
