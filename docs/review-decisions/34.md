# Review decisions of pull request #34

The history is walked slowly, and the Conversations and Runs pages show it. First round of the four agents, on `e3384d8`.

## R-34-1 | quality | major | fixed

- **Finding**: The backfill rule exists twice (the pass and the extension's answer) and the copies differ.
- **Where**: PassRunner.cs, ConversationSelection.cs
- **Reason**: Real. The pass now calls `ConversationSelection.ToBackfill` too, with the guard on Mnemarr's positions in one place and a test where the positions cannot be asked. (The held-or-unreadable exclusion is the extension side's: a conversation that the pass holds always has turns, the server refuses one with none.)

## R-34-2 | quality | major | fixed

- **Finding**: The progress reads the list and its end under two locks, and one store read per listed conversation.
- **Where**: ConversationsController.cs
- **Reason**: The two reads are one now (`ExtensionInbox.ListWithEnd`). The reads of the store stay as they are: at most about 540 reads of a local SQLite file every 15 s, measured in the sandbox at a few milliseconds, with the page's own refresh; a counting query would add a store method for no measurable gain.

## R-34-3 | quality | minor | declined

- **Finding**: The limit of 500 is written in several places (pass, extension, validator).
- **Where**: PassRunner.cs, content.js
- **Reason**: The extension is another language and cannot import it; the places point at each other in their comments, and a test pins the extension's side (exactly 500 is whole, 501 is cut).

## R-34-7 | quality | minor | fixed

- **Reason**: The comment names the source of randomness; the loop (24 pauses of 1 to 4 s at most, a minute or two) is far under the lifetime of a tab (3 minutes) and of the round.

## R-34-8 | security | low | declined

- **Finding**: The loop that opens the pages of an answer does not check for a disconnect between two opens.
- **Where**: extension/background.js
- **Reason**: The sign-out deletes the cookies, so a page opened after it is a page of nobody; a page that was opened before it is dropped by `startedBeforeSignOut`. The loop can only open signed-out pages once a disconnect has happened.
