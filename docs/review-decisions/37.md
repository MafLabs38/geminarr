# Review decisions of pull request #37

A conversation is read whole, from its end, as far back as Geminarr needs. First round of the four agents on `82c1e3a` (R-37-1 to R-37-24), second round on `fe8e2e4`.

## R-37-1 | architecture | minor | fixed

- **Finding**: The SPEC's list of fixed values is not complete: this change adds the window, the step, the time of a read, the size of the end and of the anchor.
- **Where**: docs/SPEC.md §10.2
- **Reason**: Real. The paragraph names them now, with the two ADRs.

## R-37-2 | architecture | minor | fixed

- **Finding**: One wire field, `complete`, means two things on a `list` and on a `conversation`, with opposite defaults.
- **Where**: ExtensionPushRequest, ADR 0025
- **Reason**: Said in the ADR (a consequence): same words, the meaning is by the kind of the push, and the default of a conversation is what every extension before this one sent.

## R-37-3 | architecture | minor | fixed

- **Finding**: The ADR says that a continued conversation costs a screen or two; more than about seven new turns make the whole be read.
- **Where**: ADR 0025
- **Reason**: The consequence says it: ten turns for the end, three of them the anchor.

## R-37-4 | architecture | minor | declined

- **Finding**: The pull request carries two decisions (the amendment of ADR 0022 and D-33).
- **Where**: PR 37
- **Reason**: They are coupled (the end can be read only because a conversation is read whole, from its end, in a window), and the maintainer asked for one branch and one pull request.

## R-37-5 | quality | minor | declined

- **Finding**: The read has two exits that a `null` does not tell apart, and constants of the read are not all named.
- **Where**: extension/content.js
- **Reason**: The caller does the same for both (the page is given up, the next round tries again); `END_TURNS` is named and commented.

## R-37-6 | quality | minor | declined

- **Finding**: `IsRewritten` puts a pattern variable in the middle of a boolean chain.
- **Where**: PassRunner.cs
- **Reason**: Cosmetic: tested, and the analyzer passes it.

## R-37-7 | quality | minor | declined

- **Finding**: `FindAnchor` checks a loop-invariant guard in its condition and rehashes turns.
- **Where**: ConversationLines.cs
- **Reason**: A part holds about ten turns: the cost is nothing, and the guard keeps the loop to one line.

## R-37-8 | quality | minor | declined

- **Finding**: `Anchored` is nullable on the result and turned into an empty list by `Ok` only.
- **Where**: ExtensionPush.cs
- **Reason**: The controller writes the same empty list for the answers that do not carry any; a non-null default would change a positional record that its tests construct.

## R-37-9 | quality | minor | fixed

- **Finding**: The anchor is stored from turns masked twice and found in turns masked once: this holds only if the mask is idempotent, and no test says so.
- **Where**: SecretMasker, ExtensionService
- **Reason**: Real. A test that the mask is idempotent, and a test that an end that holds a secret joins.

## R-37-10 | quality | minor | declined

- **Finding**: `complete` has two meanings in the protocol.
- **Where**: ExtensionController
- **Reason**: See the architecture finding on the same point.

## R-37-11 | quality | minor | declined

- **Finding**: A constant sits between a comment and the constants that it describes, and `probeTab:` is a literal.
- **Where**: background.js
- **Reason**: Cosmetic: a constant placed between two comments, and a literal prefix used once, change no behaviour and no test; not worth a line of the diff.

## R-37-12 | security | low | declined

- **Finding**: Up to 25 windows can be open at once on the desktop.
- **Where**: background.js
- **Reason**: They are opened one after the other with a pause of one to four seconds, each is closed as soon as it has said what it had to say, and a window that hangs is closed by the sweep after five minutes.

## R-37-13 | security | low | declined

- **Finding**: The read of a conversation has no bound on turns, and the server refuses what is over its limits.
- **Where**: content.js
- **Reason**: The read is bounded in time (four minutes); a conversation over the limits is refused by the server with a sentence of ours, and tried again at the next round.

## R-37-14 | test | minor | declined

- **Finding**: The time bound of a read is not tested.
- **Where**: content.test.mjs
- **Reason**: It would move the clock of the page for one line of code; the bound that matters (a page that does not follow the scroll) is tested.

## R-37-15 | test | minor | declined

- **Finding**: The condition of a turn that waits for Mnemarr is not exercised.
- **Where**: ExtensionServiceFixture.cs
- **Reason**: `unsent` covers a turn known and not sent, which is what that condition says; the outbox is read by a method of the same store that other tests cover.

## R-37-16 | test | minor | declined

- **Finding**: `IsRewritten` is not run with an offset.
- **Where**: PassRunnerFixture.cs
- **Reason**: A rewritten turn inside the anchor makes the part not join, so the pass is given the whole conversation, which the other tests cover; with an offset the last known turn is inside the anchor.

## R-37-17 | test | minor | declined

- **Finding**: The restoration of `display` is not asserted.
- **Where**: content.test.mjs
- **Reason**: The text of the turn is what the page gives back; the restoration is the `finally` of a six-line function.

## R-37-18 | test | minor | declined

- **Finding**: Nothing checks the wire names between the two sides.
- **Where**: ExtensionApiFixture.cs, background.test.mjs
- **Reason**: The end-to-end journey runs the real extension against the real Geminarr, which is what proves the names; the sandbox read three ends on the real page.

## R-37-19 | test | minor | declined

- **Finding**: A test is named for two cases and exercises one.
- **Where**: ExtensionServiceFixture.cs
- **Reason**: The name says that a conversation that does not say is taken whole; the other case is the default of the same code path.

## R-37-20 | test | minor | fixed

- **Finding**: The security branches of `part` have no test.
- **Where**: background.test.mjs
- **Reason**: A test: an end that a page read before the sign-out handed over is not taken.

## R-37-21 | test | minor | declined

- **Finding**: The 1 500-turn test may be slow.
- **Where**: content.test.mjs
- **Reason**: It runs in about a second.

## R-37-22 | test | minor | declined

- **Finding**: The risk accepted in the ADR (three identical turns in a row) is not pinned, and a negative length or an empty end is not covered.
- **Where**: ConversationAnchorFixture.cs
- **Reason**: Said in the ADR as accepted; an empty end finds nothing, and a length of nothing is tested.

## R-37-23 | test | minor | declined

- **Finding**: A part given for a conversation that is not known, with Mnemarr holding less, is not tested.
- **Where**: PassRunnerFixture.cs
- **Reason**: A part is asked for only for a conversation that Geminarr holds, and the pass refuses one that does not reach back (tested).

## R-37-24 | architecture | major | fixed

- **Finding**: The four-minute limit of a read creates a failure that nobody sees and that repeats at every round.
- **Where**: extension/content.js, background.js, ExtensionService.cs
- **Reason**: Real, and fixed as a state: the extension says `unfinished`, Geminarr marks the conversation in error (`last_error` `unfinished-read`, shown on the Conversations page), keeps what it held, sends nothing in part and does not ask for it again for six hours; the runbook and ADR 0025 say what it is; tests on both sides and on the page.

## R-37-25 | architecture | minor | fixed

- **Finding**: The `anchor` column is missing from the data model of the SPEC.
- **Where**: docs/SPEC.md
- **Reason**: Added, with the reason `unfinished-read`.

## R-37-26 | architecture | minor | declined

- **Finding**: The anchor is written by the pass for every reader and matched by the service of the extension.
- **Where**: ExtensionService.cs, PassRunner.cs
- **Reason**: The anchor is a function of `ConversationLines` (Core) that both use; the only reader that reads from the end is the extension's, and the ADR says so.

## R-37-27 | architecture | minor | fixed

- **Finding**: `probe?` reads the mark of a tab without checking that it is a page of the extension's own.
- **Where**: background.js
- **Reason**: `isProbeTab` checks both, and `part` uses the same check (the mark and the permission are one).

## R-37-28 | architecture | minor | declined

- **Finding**: Conversation windows appear on the desktop that the operator signs in through.
- **Where**: ADR 0022
- **Reason**: Said in the amendment: a window for the seconds that a conversation takes, after the sign-in; and a page of the extension takes no input now (see the security finding).

## R-37-29 | quality | minor | declined

- **Finding**: Each call of the read has its own budget of four minutes, so a page can take eight.
- **Where**: content.js, background.js
- **Reason**: The end read first holds ten turns (a few steps); the tab of the extension is closed by the sweep after five minutes whatever happens.

## R-37-30 | quality | minor | declined

- **Finding**: The anchor is stored over the last three turns of the whole and looked up over `min(3, known)`.
- **Where**: PassRunner.cs, ExtensionService.cs
- **Reason**: They are the same number: an end that is given holds at least the three turns of the anchor or the whole conversation (ADR 0025).

## R-37-31 | quality | minor | declined

- **Finding**: The branch where the part does not reach back saves the record with no anchor and logs nothing.
- **Where**: PassRunner.cs
- **Reason**: The next reading is a whole one, which renews the anchor; a line in the log would say nothing the Runs page does not.

## R-37-32 | quality | minor | declined

- **Finding**: `IsRewritten` is hard to read.
- **Where**: PassRunner.cs
- **Reason**: The agent agrees that it is not worth a change on its own.

## R-37-33 | security | medium | fixed

- **Finding**: Keystrokes of a person at the desktop could go into a conversation window, which would be a prompt written to the assistant.
- **Where**: extension/content.js, ADR 0025
- **Reason**: Real, and fixed whatever the window manager does: a page that the extension opened swallows every key, paste and drop (capture phase) and lets go of the prompt box; a page that a person opened is left alone; tests on both.

## R-37-34 | security | low | fixed

- **Finding**: `part` does not check that the tab was marked to read the end of its conversation.
- **Where**: background.js
- **Reason**: `part` requires the mark (`isProbeTab`); a test for a page of its own that was not marked.

## R-37-35 | test | minor | declined

- **Finding**: The condition of a turn waiting for Mnemarr, the boundary `sent == first`, an offset in `IsRewritten`, an anchor shorter than three, the shape of the JSON of `anchored`, the wire names.
- **Where**: several tests
- **Reason**: Covered in kind by the tests that exist (unsent, a part that does not reach back, an end that joins, a conversation shorter than the anchor) and by the end-to-end journey, which runs the real extension against Geminarr.

## R-37-36 | test | minor | fixed

- **Finding**: Idempotence of the mask is tested for one token shape only.
- **Where**: SecretMaskerFixture.cs
- **Reason**: A theory over every shared sample.

## R-37-37 | test | minor | declined

- **Finding**: The expiry test of a tab no longer proves the boundary, the long tests may be slow, double parentheses in two tests.
- **Where**: several tests
- **Reason**: Shape of the tests: the boundary is the constant, the long tests run in about a second, the parentheses are what the formatter leaves.
